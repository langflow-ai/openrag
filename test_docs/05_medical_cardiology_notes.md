# Clinical Cardiology & Diagnostic Electrocardiography

Clinical management of cardiac arrhythmias, ischemic heart diseases, and myocardial infarction protocols in acute hospital settings.

## Cardiac Arrhythmias & Atrial Fibrillation
Atrial fibrillation (AFib) is characterized by disorganized atrial electrical activity leading to an irregular ventricular response. Electrocardiogram (ECG/EKG) findings typically display absent discrete P-waves replaced by irregular fibrillatory baseline waves with irregular R-R intervals.

### Diagnostic Criteria and Risk Stratification
1. Calculate **CHA2DS2-VASc** score to assess thromboembolic stroke risk:
   - Congestive heart failure (1 pt)
   - Hypertension (1 pt)
   - Age $\ge$ 75 years (2 pts)
   - Diabetes mellitus (1 pt)
   - Prior stroke / TIA (2 pts)
   - Vascular disease (1 pt)
   - Age 65–74 years (1 pt)
   - Sex category (female = 1 pt)

2. Anticoagulation therapy (DOACs like apixaban or dabigatran) is indicated for non-valvular AFib in patients with elevated scores.

## Pharmacological Interventions
- **Beta-Blockers**: Metoprolol and carvedilol for rate control and reducing myocardial oxygen demand.
- **Calcium Channel Blockers**: Diltiazem and verapamil for slowing AV nodal conduction.
- **Antiarrhythmics**: Amiodarone for refractory rhythm management and ventricular tachycardia prophylaxis.
