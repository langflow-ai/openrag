# Culinary Techniques & French Sauces

A comprehensive guide to foundational kitchen practices, knife skills, classical French mother sauces, and traditional pastry baking.

## The Five Mother Sauces
1. **Béchamel**: Roux whisked with warm milk, seasoned with white pepper and nutmeg.
2. **Velouté**: Light blond roux combined with chicken, veal, or fish stock.
3. **Espagnole**: Brown roux enriched with roasted veal stock, mirepoix, and tomato purée.
4. **Sauce Tomat**: Rendered salt pork, aromatic vegetables, tomatoes, and gentle simmering.
5. **Hollandaise**: Warm emulsion of egg yolks, clarified butter, and fresh lemon juice reduction.

## Traditional Apple Pie Pastry
For an authentic rustic apple pie, prepare a flaky butter crust (pâte brisée):
- 300g unbleached all-purpose flour
- 200g cold unsalted European butter, cubed into small dice
- 5g fine sea salt
- 60ml ice-cold water

Filling Preparation:
Toss 6 peeled Granny Smith and Honeycrisp apple slices with 50g granulated cane sugar, 10g ground cinnamon, 2g grated nutmeg, and 15ml fresh lemon juice. Bake at 190°C (375°F) for 45 minutes until the pastry crust turns golden brown and the fruit filling bubbles continuously.

## Knife Maintenance and Sharpening
Maintain high-carbon Japanese chef knives with regular honing on a ceramic rod and water stone sharpening (1000 grit for edge setting, 6000 grit for mirror polishing). Always wash kitchen cutlery by hand and dry thoroughly to avoid oxidation.
