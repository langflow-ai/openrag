IBM Elite Support offers enterprise support for BomaRAG to help you develop and deploy BomaRAG applications with confidence.

IBM Elite Support for BomaRAG is a subscription to the BomaRAG expertise at IBM. It's meant for BomaRAG users who want all the benefits of running their own open-source deployments, as well as the peace of mind that comes with having direct access to the team that has authored the majority of the BomaRAG code.

IBM Elite Support subscribers can get help with general-purpose and technical questions for their open-source BomaRAG deployments. If an issue is encountered, IBM is there to help.

To subscribe or learn more, see [IBM Elite Support for BomaRAG](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/announcements/elite-support-bomarag-delivers-enterprise-support-users-need-confidently-create-deploy-bomarag-applications).
