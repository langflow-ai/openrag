# French Onion Soup

Caramelise thinly sliced onions for 45-60 minutes over low heat, stirring occasionally. Patience is the key tip: high heat makes onions bitter instead of sweet. Add a pinch of sugar if the onions are not naturally sweet. Deglaze the pan with white wine or cognac, scraping up the browned bits, then add beef broth and simmer for 20 minutes. Ladle into oven-safe bowls, top with toasted baguette slices and grated gruyère, and broil until the cheese is bubbling and golden.
