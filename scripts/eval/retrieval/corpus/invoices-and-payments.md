# Invoices and payments

This page covers how Relaywise charges you, where to find invoices, and what happens when a payment does not go through. For what each plan costs and includes, see the plans page.

## When you are charged

The billing period starts on the day you first moved to a paid plan and repeats monthly or yearly from that date. On each renewal date Relaywise charges:

- the plan fee for the coming period, in advance, and
- any overage from the period that has just ended, in arrears.

An invoice is issued on the renewal date and emailed to the billing contact.

## Payment methods

Paid plans can be paid by credit or debit card. Workspaces on a yearly Scale plan and all Enterprise workspaces can ask to pay by bank transfer against an invoice. Bank transfer invoices are due within 14 days of the invoice date.

To change the card, open **Settings → Billing → Payment method**. The new card is used from the next charge. Only Owners can see or change payment details.

## Finding invoices

All invoices are listed under **Settings → Billing → Invoices**. Each one can be downloaded as a PDF. An invoice shows the plan fee, overage with the number of extra events, any discount, tax, and the total.

## The billing contact

Invoices and payment notices go to the billing contact, which is the first Owner unless you change it. Add a shared mailbox, such as your accounts department, under **Settings → Billing → Contacts**. You can list up to five addresses.

## Tax

Enter your company's tax registration number under **Settings → Billing → Tax details**. The number appears on future invoices. Depending on where your company is registered, a valid number may change how tax is applied. Invoices that have already been issued are not regenerated; ask support if you need a corrected copy.

## When a payment fails

Cards are declined for ordinary reasons: an expired card, a spending limit, a bank's fraud check. When a charge fails:

1. Relaywise emails the billing contact and the Owners immediately.
2. The charge is tried again 3 days, 5 days and 7 days after the first failure.
3. During this time the workspace works normally.
4. If the charge has still not succeeded 10 days after the first failure, the workspace is **suspended**.

A suspended workspace rejects new events and makes no deliveries. Configuration and history stay in place. Paying the outstanding invoice lifts the suspension within a few minutes.

If the invoice is still unpaid 30 days after suspension, the workspace is closed and its data is deleted.

## Refunds

Monthly plans are not refunded for a period that has started; cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Yearly plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days a yearly plan runs to the end of its term.

If you were charged overage because of a fault on the Relaywise side, such as a replay that ran twice, contact support and the extra events will be credited.

## Credits

Credits from a refund, a goodwill gesture or a service-level payout are held on the workspace and applied automatically to the next invoices until they are used up. The balance is shown at the top of the **Invoices** page. Credits cannot be paid out as cash.

## Purchase orders

Enterprise customers can add a purchase order number to their invoices. Send the number to your account contact before the renewal date.
